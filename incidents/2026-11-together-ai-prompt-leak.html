The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 12:49 PM ET

Axios reports OpenRouter's customer emails are genuine and named three providers that forwarded requests to MiniMax.

How big is it?

Regional. Together AI has not said how many customers or prompts were affected. The stored samples came from test requests to three models on four main days in June and July. The same week, OpenRouter dropped three providers over related findings.

Who is affected?

Together AI customers whose serverless requests to MiniMax M3, GLM-5.2 or Kimi K2.6 were sampled, mainly on Jun 24, Jul 4, Jul 8 and Jul 14. The company says it will contact affected customers directly.

Known so far

  • An unauthorized party downloaded stored prompt samples from Together AI's serverless service. evidence
  • The way in was a credential for a private repository, committed by an AI coding agent to a public one. evidence
  • The samples were from requests mainly on Jun 24, Jul 4, Jul 8 and Jul 14, 2026. evidence
  • The party searched the prompts for credentials and tested them; five were Dragos bait keys. evidence
  • OpenRouter dropped three providers and removed 125 endpoints from its zero-data-retention list, and says it was not breached. evidence

Not known yet

  • Who the unauthorized party was.
  • How many customers or prompts were affected.
  • Whether the downloaded data was deleted or shared further.
  • Whether real customer credentials were used.
  • How long the credential was exposed.
  • Whether the Together AI intruder is tied to the other compromised providers.

Time from the post metadata.
