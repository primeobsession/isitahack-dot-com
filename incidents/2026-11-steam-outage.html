What happened?

8
ConfirmedSteam had a broad outage on Tuesday evening, Nov 17, that took down online featuresOn Nov 17, Steam downloads, the Friends list, cloud saves and the Store were unreachable for many users, with 'No Connection' errors. XDA's writer says he saw it first-hand.

Why it supports XDA's first-hand check, an independent outlet (IT Home), and an independent tracker agree on the outage.

XDA says it could not download games, its Friends list was gone, cloud saves would not load and the store was limited. IT Home reports the same features down and says third-party monitors showed Steam services dropping sharply. Instatus's tracker page shows a spike starting about 5 PM ET (10 PM UTC) and no incident posted by Steam.

Size
Large. Steam is the largest PC game store, and the Nov 17 outage drew tens of thousands of user reports and took down downloads, the Store and cloud saves. The later reports are in the low thousands. Valve has not said how many people were affected.
Who is affected
Steam users on Tuesday evening, Nov 17, who could not download, use Friends or load the Store, and users who reported sign-in, connection or checkout problems from late Friday, Nov 20, into Saturday, Nov 21. The Tuesday outage appears fixed; the later reports looked ongoing.
