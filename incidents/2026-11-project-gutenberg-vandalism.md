# Was Project Gutenberg's website hacked?

**Confirmed.** Yes, by its own account: the foundation says outside attackers vandalized the site, injected code and got root access, then returned on Monday. It calls this “vandalism” and has not said what was taken.

## At a glance

- Status: monitoring
- Size: Large. A free library of tens of thousands of public-domain books, used worldwide. The site has been down since Nov 15-16, but the foundation says the book files appear clear. Volunteers run recovery.
- Who is affected: Readers who use gutenberg.org, and sites and tools that link to or load books from it. Contributors who use ibiblio logins cannot upload. The books appear unaffected.

- The foundation says gutenberg.org was hit by vandalism and is being rebuilt.
- Its updates describe outside attackers, injected code and root access to a server.
- As of 5:12 AM ET on Nov 21, gutenberg.org still shows a 503 maintenance page.
- ibiblio, which hosts Gutenberg services, reports its own unplanned outage that began on Nov 15.
