Was it a hack? What has been said

3
ConfirmedCHU de la Guadeloupe was hit by a cyberattackThe hospital's director general says a cyberattack began Saturday evening, Nov 21, blocking IT and phones. Staff first thought it was an IT outage, then found encrypted databases, which he says proves a deliberate attack.

Why it supports Separate outlets each report the hospital director stating on the record that the hospital is the victim of a cyberattack.

All four outlets report the director's on-record statements, made in separate interviews and to AFP. He says the cause "is not accidental" and is "linked to an aggression". The hospital's national cybersecurity agency contact says the attack is serious (see c007).

What happened?

3
ConfirmedDatabases were encrypted and are not accessibleThe director says some of the hospital's data was encrypted and is no longer accessible, and the hospital will restart software and databases one by one after checking no malware remains.

Why it supports Three outlets quote the director on encrypted, inaccessible data.

La 1ère says a part of the databases was encrypted. France-Antilles quotes the director saying data was encrypted and medical files cannot be reached. Encryption does not by itself show ransomware; see c008.

Nov 21 About 9 PM Saturday, Nov 21 (local)Phones, imaging, non-urgent lab work and software go down at the hospital, per RCI; staff first take it for an IT outageConfirmed
