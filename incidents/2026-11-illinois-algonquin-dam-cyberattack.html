Was the Algonquin Dam on the Fox River hacked?

Also reported as the Algonquin Dam hack and Fox River dam cyberattack.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Reported

Illinois DNR calls it an “apparent cyberattack”, an attempt at unauthorized access that staff blocked, and says the dam kept running. It has given no technical detail, so the attack itself is not independently shown.

Rests on Illinois DNR says staff spotted an “apparent cyberattack”, an attempt at unauthorized access, at the Algonquin Dam on Monday, Nov 16 · IDNR says staff blocked unauthorized access and the dam kept operating · Attackers gained access to the dam's control or operating systems
