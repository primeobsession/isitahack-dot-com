# Was the KEPCO customer data breach a hack?

**Confirmed.** KEPCO's own notices say an outside cyber intrusion leaked data on about 1.52 million users of its KEPCO ON and SRM systems. How the attacker got in, and who it was, are not established.

## At a glance

- Status: monitoring
- Size: Large. KEPCO, South Korea's state-run power utility, says 1,520,248 user records were leaked across two systems; the count of distinct people is not given.
- Who is affected: Users of KEPCO ON (electricity bills and payment) and of the SRM procurement system, including company representatives and staff. KEPCO has a desk to check whether you were affected.

### Known so far

- KEPCO says a cyber intrusion caused the leak of customers' personal data.
- KEPCO detected outside illegal access attempts on Nov 20, blocked them and asked police to investigate.
- KEPCO is investigating with the National Intelligence Service, the energy ministry and outside experts.
