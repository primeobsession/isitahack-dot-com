How much do you want to read?

The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 10:16 PM ET

Samsung Securities says an outsider took files with data of 8,241 clients. Reported on its statement; method and attacker unknown.

How big is it?

Regional. Samsung Securities says 8,241 clients had data in the affected files. The potential reach is wider if the count or scope changes; the company says other customer systems were not affected and is still checking.

Who is affected?

Clients who filed a VOC (customer feedback) submission with an attachment. The company says it will notify them one by one and others are not involved.

Known so far

Nothing is established yet.

Not known yet

  • How many clients were affected, beyond the company's own count. open question
  • Who was behind it. open question
  • How the intruder got in. open question
  • Whether it is linked to the Samsung Ads vendor breach. open question
