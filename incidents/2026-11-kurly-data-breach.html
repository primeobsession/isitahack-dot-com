Was Kurly hacked in the Nov 2026 data breach?

Also reported as Kurly data leak and Market Kurly personal information leak.

How much do you want to read?

The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 12:15 PM ET

Kurly says an outside attacker used stolen logins to reach its systems and about 10,900 people's data leaked; attacker Not established.

How big is it?

Regional. About 10,900 people, by Kurly's own counts: 10,699 curators, 199 current and former employees and 4 customers. Some curators' bank and ID numbers were included. Revisit if Kurly or a regulator gives new numbers.

Who is affected?

Kurly's 'curators' (people who promote Kurly products on social media for a fee), 199 current and former employees, and 4 ordinary customers. Kurly says it is notifying them. Be wary of calls, texts or emails asking for bank or ID details.

Known so far

Nothing is established yet.

Not known yet

  • Who the attacker was and how the login credentials were stolen. open question
  • How many curators had bank or ID numbers exposed, and whether those were encrypted. open question
  • Whether the stolen data has been published or misused. open question
  • Whether the privacy regulator has opened an investigation. open question
