The latest · Nov 24, 2026, 10:29 AM ET

Hugendubel's own website now calls it a cyberattack and says no sensitive customer data is affected so far. Its shop is still offline.

Is it back?

Down at our last check. The Hugendubel web shop, the tolino cloud and the service center are offline. evidence Checked Nov 24, 2026, 10:28 AM ET

How big is it?

Regional. A major German bookseller with more than 100 stores has its web shop, service center, email and tolino e-book cloud offline for days. Number of affected customers not published.

Who is affected?

Hugendubel and ebook.de web shop customers and tolino e-reader owners who buy through them. Books already on a device still open; new downloads, sync and Family Sharing do not work. heise reports that other tolino shops such as Thalia are not affected.

Known so far

  • A Hugendubel spokesperson told dpa it was a hacker attack in the night of Nov 18 to 19. evidence
  • The company's own website notice now says it was the target of a cyberattack. Until Nov 23 it said only 'technical disruption'. evidence
  • The notice says that, as of Nov 24, no sensitive or personal customer data is affected. That is the company's own statement. evidence
  • The web shop, the tolino cloud and the service center are offline. evidence
  • Tolino cloud downloads of books not already on a device, reading-progress sync and Family Sharing do not work. evidence
  • Free return labels are available again through a link on Hugendubel's help page, and return deadlines are extended. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether it was ransomware. open question
  • Who the attacker was. open question
  • How the attackers got in. open question
  • When the shop and tolino cloud will be back. open question
