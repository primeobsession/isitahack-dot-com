Was the Hugendubel outage a hack?

Also reported as the Hugendubel hack and the tolino cloud outage.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, per the company: a spokesperson told dpa it was a hacker attack on Nov 18 to 19. Its own web notices say only 'technical disruption'. No outside confirmation yet. Attacker, method and ransomware are not established.

Rests on Hugendubel says it was hit by a hacker attack on its IT systems in the night of Nov 18 to 19, 2026 · Hugendubel's own website notice and help pages call the outage a 'technical disruption' and do not mention an attack
