- A third party copied payment card data stored on Cleverbridge's platform.
- Cleverbridge detected the activity on Nov 4 and had systems back online that day.
- Cleverbridge began notifying affected people on Nov 20.
- The company says it was not ransomware: no systems were encrypted.
