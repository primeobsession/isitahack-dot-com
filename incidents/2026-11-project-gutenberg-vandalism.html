Was Project Gutenberg's website hacked?

Also reported as the Gutenberg hack and Project Gutenberg down.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, by its own account: the foundation says outside attackers vandalized the site, injected code and got root access, then returned on Monday. It calls this “vandalism” and has not said what was taken.

Rests on The foundation says gutenberg.org was hit by vandalism and is being rebuilt · Outside attackers broke into Project Gutenberg's systems, including root access to a server

This is the current answer, not a final verdict. A final verdict is published only after the site's owner has reviewed the evidence.
