Was the RIKEN Fugaku supercomputer break-in a hack?

Also reported as the Fugaku hack and the RIKEN supercomputer cyberattack.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, by RIKEN's own account: an outside party got into Fugaku's operational network from Nov 10 to 13, installed malicious programs on some servers and took users' files, including private keys. Who did it is not known.

Rests on An outside party got into Fugaku's operational network · Malicious programs were installed on some servers · 430 stolen private keys for outside systems, 233 with no passphrase

How did they get in?

2
ConfirmedThe way in was the public operating-status dashboardRIKEN says the intruder used a publicly reachable web page that displays Fugaku's operating status (the dashboard) to get into the internal network.

Why it supports RIKEN's first notice names the dashboard as the entry point.

RIKEN says the exact method is still being investigated. Its first notice says it will fix vulnerabilities in the software that was exploited before restarting, but it has not named the software or the flaw. The page has been taken down, according to the Japan Times.
