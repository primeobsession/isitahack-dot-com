The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 11:12 AM ET

Atlassian's Bitbucket and sign-in outage has been going since 10:19 AM ET on Nov 23. Atlassian has not said what caused it.

How big is it?

Large. Potential reach is broad among developers: Bitbucket and Atlassian sign-in are widely used by software teams. Atlassian lists only a partial outage, and it had run about an hour at last check. It has not said how many customers were hit.

Who is affected?

Developers and teams who sign in through Atlassian's OAuth service or run Bitbucket Pipelines, from 10:19 AM ET on Monday, Nov 23. The incident was still open at our last check.

Known so far

  • Atlassian's Bitbucket status page opened an incident at 10:19 AM ET on Nov 23 for failing OAuth token requests, and it was still open at 11:12 AM ET. evidence
  • The incident lists Authentication and user management and Pipelines as partial outages; the Bitbucket website, API and Git access show operational. evidence
  • Atlassian says it has identified the cause and is working on a mitigation. It has not said what the cause is. evidence

Not known yet

  • What caused the outage. Atlassian has not said.
  • Whether it was an attack. No one has claimed one and Atlassian has not addressed it.
  • How many customers were affected.
  • When it will be fixed.

Not verified: initial investigation only. Nothing on this page is absolutely confirmed. It is an early look based only on publicly available sources, and it should not be treated as verified fact. Even items labeled "Confirmed" only mean a public source reports them.
