The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 1:35 PM ET

Atlassian has now posted the sign-in outage on Confluence's status page too; it is still recovering, not resolved.

Is it back?

Down at our last check. Atlassian opened a Bitbucket status incident at 10:19 AM ET on Nov 23 for failing OAuth token requests. evidence Checked Nov 23, 2026, 1:35 PM ET

How big is it?

Large. Potential reach is broad: the failing sign-in service is shared by Bitbucket, Jira, JSM, Trello and Confluence, which are widely used by software and business teams. Atlassian lists only partial outages, applied a fix about 75 minutes in, and has not said how many customers were hit.

Who is affected?

People and teams who sign in to Bitbucket, Jira, JSM, Trello or Confluence through Atlassian's OAuth service, or run Bitbucket Pipelines, from 10:19 AM ET on Monday, Nov 23. Atlassian reported signs of recovery from 11:33 AM ET; the incident was not marked resolved at our last check.

Known so far

  • Atlassian's Bitbucket status page opened an incident at 10:19 AM ET on Nov 23 for failing OAuth token requests, and it was still open at 1:35 PM ET. evidence
  • On Bitbucket, Authentication and user management and Pipelines are listed as partial outages; the website, API and Git access show operational. evidence
  • The same sign-in incident is posted on Atlassian's Jira, Jira Service Management, Trello, Developer and Confluence status pages. evidence
  • Atlassian says it identified the cause at 11:08 AM ET and applied a mitigation at 11:33 AM ET, with signs of recovery. It has not said what the cause is. evidence

Not known yet

  • What caused the outage. Atlassian has not said.
  • Whether it was an attack. No one has claimed one and Atlassian has not addressed it.
  • How many customers were affected.
  • When it will be marked fully resolved. Atlassian was still monitoring recovery at our last check.
