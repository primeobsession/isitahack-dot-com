What we checked and did not find

We looked for these and did not find them as of the time of this report on Nov 22, 2026 (last checked 12:19 PM ET).

  • The number of affected cards or people, including any filing on the California attorney general's public breach list.
  • A list of businesses whose customers were affected, from Cleverbridge or the businesses.
  • A company statement on exactly which card details were taken, or why customer notices differ (some list the CVV/CVC, others say it was not copied).
  • How the third party got in, how long they were there, or any technical write-up.
  • Any attacker named or claim of responsibility.
  • A statement from a regulator or card network.
  • Any report of the copied cards being used for fraud.
