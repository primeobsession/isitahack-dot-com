The latest · Nov 21, 2026, 12:35 AM ET

OPM still has not commented on the reported USA Staffing access, and no second outlet has confirmed it.

How big is it?

Local. The report says 403 tentative job offers across multiple federal agencies. USA Staffing is used by many agencies, but no source says more than those offers were reached.

Who is affected?

People with tentative federal job offers made through USA Staffing in the affected period, per the report. Those affected were reportedly notified. If you are unsure, ask your hiring agency.

Known so far

  • USA Staffing is OPM's hiring system for federal agencies. evidence
  • OPM has published no statement about this as of Nov 21. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether the access happened as described. It rests on one outlet's account of an internal email.
  • How the outside party got in and who they were.
  • Exactly what data was exposed and to whom.
  • Whether it has any link to the FAA ground stop or the Microsoft outage. No source gives one.
  • Whether OPM will make a public statement.

Not verified: initial investigation only. Nothing on this page is absolutely confirmed. It is an early look based only on publicly available sources, and it should not be treated as verified fact. Even items labeled "Confirmed" only mean a public source reports them.
