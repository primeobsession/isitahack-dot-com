What happened?

1
ConfirmedDropbox's status page opened an incident at 2:28 PM ET on Nov 20 and marked it resolved at 3:13 PM ETDropbox posted an incident titled 'Dropbox is not working as expected for some users' at 2:28 PM ET (19:28 UTC) on Friday, Nov 20. It said a fix was in place at 3:05 PM and marked the incident resolved at 3:13 PM, about 45 minutes in all.

Dropbox's updates were: Investigating (2:28 PM), a further update (2:37 PM), Monitoring (3:05 PM), Resolved (3:13 PM). The times are the status page's own, converted from UTC. The page gives no detail beyond that.
