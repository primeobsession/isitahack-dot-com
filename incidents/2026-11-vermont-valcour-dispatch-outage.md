## At a glance

- Status: monitoring
- Size: Regional. Valcour runs dispatch and records for nearly all Vermont police and many fire and EMS agencies, 97 agencies by one count. It is statewide, but 911 still works and no use outside Vermont is known.
- Who is affected: Vermont police, fire and EMS agencies, which are working on paper, radio and phone. The public can still call 911 and non-emergency lines. Some records work, such as reports, may be slower.

### Known so far

- The vendor told the state late on Nov 16 of a potential security incident, and the state took Valcour offline as a precaution.
- 911 and emergency response still work.
- The state has notified the Vermont Attorney General's Office and the FBI.
- Valcour stays offline until the investigation ends and any weaknesses are fixed. There is no timeline.
