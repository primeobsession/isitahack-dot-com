## At a glance

- Status: monitoring
- Size: Regional. A major German bookseller with more than 100 stores has its web shop, service center, email and tolino e-book cloud offline for days. Number of affected customers not published.
- Who is affected: Hugendubel and ebook.de web shop customers and tolino e-reader owners who buy through them. Books already on a device still open; new downloads, sync and Family Sharing do not work. heise reports that other tolino shops such as Thalia are not affected.

### Known so far

- A Hugendubel spokesperson told dpa it was a hacker attack in the night of Nov 18 to 19.
- The company's own website notice now says it was the target of a cyberattack. Until Nov 23 it said only 'technical disruption'.
- The notice says that, as of Nov 24, no sensitive or personal customer data is affected. That is the company's own statement.
- The web shop, the tolino cloud and the service center are offline.
- Tolino cloud downloads of books not already on a device, reading-progress sync and Family Sharing do not work.
- Free return labels are available again through a link on Hugendubel's help page, and return deadlines are extended.

### Not known yet

- Whether it was ransomware.
- Who the attacker was.
- How the attackers got in.
- When the shop and tolino cloud will be back.
