# Was the Docomo customer data leak a hack?

**Confirmed.** Yes. Docomo says outside parties got into My docomo and its online shop, and that customer information leaked. Jiji Press and Nippon TV report the same. The cause and the attacker are not established.

Incident: NTT Docomo customer data breach, Nov 2026. Also reported as the Docomo data leak and the My docomo hack. First published Nov 22, 2026; last updated Nov 22, 2026, 4:53 AM ET.

## At a glance

- Status: monitoring
- Size: Large. Up to about 300,000 phone lines, per Docomo; two outlets report the briefing figure of 294,839 (204,157 personal).
- Who is affected: Docomo customers, personal and corporate, whose data was held by My docomo and the online shop, including some who had already cancelled.

### Known so far

- Docomo says outside parties got into My docomo and the online shop.
- Customer information leaked, for up to about 300,000 lines.
- Names, birth dates, contact details, ID-document numbers and contract details leaked; for some, bank details.
- My docomo is partly back as of Nov 22; the online shop and the iOS app are still in emergency maintenance.

### Not known yet

- Who the attacker was.
- The full root cause.
- Whether the leaked data has been misused or sold.
- How much compensation will be paid, and to whom.
- When the online shop and iOS app return.
