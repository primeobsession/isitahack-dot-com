The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 7:11 PM ET

Bell MTS says a cyberattack caused its email outage. One outlet so far; Reported.

Is it back?

Back. Bell MTS customers in Manitoba were locked out of email for days; Bell said it was restored Nov 20. evidence Checked Nov 22, 2026, 7:11 PM ET

How big is it?

Regional. Bell MTS email customers across Manitoba were locked out for more than two days, per CBC and the Free Press. Bell has not said how many customers or accounts were affected, so size rests on reach, not a count.

Who is affected?

Bell MTS email customers in Manitoba, many locked out from about Nov 17 to Nov 20 and some for longer. Bell says it will contact customers whose data may have been affected.

Known so far

  • Bell MTS email customers in Manitoba were locked out for days, and Bell said on Nov 20 that service was restored. evidence
  • Bell gave no cause for the outage until Nov 22. evidence
  • Bell says an agentic attacker stole enterprise customer data from its systems on Nov 17. evidence
  • Bell says it shared details with the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security and is working with the RCMP's cybercrime unit. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether a second outlet or Bell's own notice carries the cyberattack statement.
  • How many customers or accounts were affected, and what data was reachable. open question
  • Who was behind it. open question
  • Whether it is the same attack as Bell Canada's Nov 17 breach. open question
