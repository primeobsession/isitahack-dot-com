Was the KEPCO customer data breach a hack?

Also reported as the KEPCO ON leak and KEPCO's second breach.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Reported

KEPCO says an outside attacker broke into its KEPCO ON and SRM systems and stole data on about 1.52 million people. We read this only via outlets, so it stays Reported. Method and attacker are not established.

Rests on KEPCO says an outside attacker broke into two of its systems and took data · About 1.52 million people's data was leaked across the two systems

This is the current answer, not a final verdict. A final verdict is published only after the site's owner has reviewed the evidence.

ReportedKEPCO says an outside attacker broke into two of its systems and took dataKEPCO announced on Nov 22 that it confirmed a leak of personal data from its KEPCO ON customer platform and its SRM procurement system, and said an external attacker accessed both systems and stole the data.

Why it supports Five outlets report KEPCO's statement; none adds an independent check, and the company's own notice was not read.

Every outlet we read relays KEPCO's own statement; we did not read KEPCO's notice itself. In the Korean reports the company's wording is that the attacker is 'presumed' to have broken in and taken the data (Yonhap, Chosunbiz, Financial News). Outlets repeating one source count as one.
