The latest · Nov 21, 2026, 4:35 PM ET

Airbyte's X posts now linked directly; no new facts.

Is it back?

Partly back at our last check. As of 1:00 AM ET Nov 21, Airbyte said 15 API connectors, Connector Builder and MCP were still down 'as a cautionary measure'. evidence Checked Nov 21, 2026, 4:35 PM ET

How big is it?

Regional. Airbyte Cloud moves data between many companies' apps and databases, and every customer lost service for days. Airbyte says fewer than 1% of customers were directly affected. Salesforce also cut access. The full count is unknown.

Who is affected?

Companies that use Airbyte Cloud to sync data. API connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk and others were still down as of 1:00 AM ET Nov 21. Affected customers were contacted by Airbyte; others need only watch its status page.

Known so far

  • Airbyte says a security incident gave an attacker access to its systems; it blocked them and took Cloud offline on Nov 17. evidence
  • Airbyte Cloud was offline for days; the service began coming back on Nov 19. evidence
  • Airbyte says fewer than 1% of its customers were affected and that it contacted them. evidence
  • As of 1:00 AM ET Nov 21, 15 API connectors, Connector Builder and MCP were still down as a cautionary measure. evidence
  • Salesforce disabled the Airbyte connected app on Nov 20 and says this was not a Salesforce platform problem. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether customer data, credentials or metadata were accessed or taken.
  • How the attacker got in. Airbyte says a full incident report is coming.
  • Who is behind it, and what 'broader, global cyberattack' Airbyte means.
  • Whether Salesforce or other platforms' customer data was reached through Airbyte.
  • When the remaining 15 API connectors, Builder and MCP will return.
  • Whether self-managed Airbyte installs were affected.
