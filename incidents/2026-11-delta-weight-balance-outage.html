The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 4:46 PM ET

ABC's updated count: more than 1,600 Delta flights delayed, per Delta.

How big is it?

Large. More than 1,600 Delta flights were delayed on Nov 22, per ABC citing Delta, during the start of Thanksgiving travel. Delta says about 5,000 flights were scheduled that day.

Who is affected?

Delta and Delta Connection passengers on Sunday, Nov 22, mainly at Atlanta and on flights into it. Delta says it is rebooking customers and offering meals, hotels and refunds.

Known so far

  • Delta's weight-and-balance automation system went down on Sunday, Nov 22; crews used manual workarounds. evidence
  • The FAA stopped Atlanta-bound Delta flights at Delta's request, then eased it to a Delta-only delay just after 8:40 AM ET. evidence

Not known yet

  • What caused the outage. Delta gives no cause.
  • Whether a cyberattack was involved. No official statement addresses it either way.
  • Whether it relates to the FAA's Nov 13 ground stop or United's Nov 16 outage.

Not verified: initial investigation only. Nothing on this page is absolutely confirmed. It is an early look based only on publicly available sources, and it should not be treated as verified fact. Even items labeled "Confirmed" only mean a public source reports them.
