Was the ZOZO data leak a hack?

Also reported as the ZOZOTOWN data leak, the WEAR data leak and the PLAID KARTE breach.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Says it was hacked

Yes, but at a supplier: an outside party got into PLAID's KARTE service on Nov 13 and took data, including customer data ZOZO kept there. Neither company says ZOZO's own systems were breached.

How much do you want to read?

The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 5:45 PM ET

GDO, Francfranc and CAMPFIRE each say millions of their own member records leaked or may have leaked via KARTE.

How big is it?

Large. About 15.18 million ZOZOTOWN users and 13.93 million WEAR users, per ZOZO. The two groups overlap by an unpublished amount. The same vendor breach hit hundreds of other companies.

Who is affected?

ZOZOTOWN and WEAR by ZOZO users, including former ZOZOTOWN members, and current and former staff of brands on ZOZOTOWN. Leaked: member ID, age, gender, postal code; for about 47,000, also display name, email and phone. Passwords were not, per ZOZO. Expect fake emails and texts.

Known so far

  • An outside party got into PLAID's KARTE service on Nov 13, and ZOZO data held there leaked. evidence
  • About 15.18M ZOZOTOWN users (former members too) had member ID, age, gender and postal code leaked. evidence
  • About 13.93M WEAR users and about 24,000 brand staff were also hit. evidence
  • Passwords were not exposed, per ZOZO. evidence
  • PLAID says the intrusion hit many other companies, and KARTE has been off since Nov 13. evidence

Not known yet

  • Who the attacker was. open question
  • Whether the data has been used against people, or whether more was taken. open question
  • Whether PLAID's account of the method holds up to independent review. open question
