### Known so far

- Journal Technologies says suspicious, malicious automated activity targeted its public portal software, first identified on Nov 13.
- The vendor advised customers to take their public portals offline, and courts did.
- Public portals or e-filing were offline at many courts, including Alameda, Kern, Marin, Ventura, Riverside, Los Angeles, Spokane, Lake County, Illinois, and King County, Washington.
- Kern's court says its portal is offline and gives no estimated restoration time.
- Riverside's court says its portal is restored and users must reset their passwords.

### Not known yet

- Whether any data was exposed; the vendor says it has found no evidence so far for Criminal Justice Information and is still investigating.
- When every affected portal will be restored, and how many courts are affected.
