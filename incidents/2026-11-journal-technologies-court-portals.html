Was the Journal Technologies court portal outage a cyberattack?

Also reported as court portal outage, California court portals down and eCourt portal outage.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, by the vendor's own account: it says malicious automated activity targeted its public portal software on Nov 13. Who did it, how far it got and whether data was taken are not established.
