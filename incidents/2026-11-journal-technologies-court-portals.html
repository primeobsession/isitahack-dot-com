The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 11:22 PM ET

A local outlet dates Riverside's portal restoration to Saturday, Nov 21; Kern, Ventura and King County are still down.

Is it back?

Down at our last check. King County's Superior Court Clerk in Washington expects its KC Script portal to stay unavailable through Nov 29, citing technical issues at its vendor. evidence Checked Nov 21, 2026, 4:30 PM ET

How big is it?

Regional. The vendor serves courts in several states and countries. Court notices and press show portals offline at many courts in California, Illinois, Nevada and Washington; the Sacramento Bee counts 20 California superior courts. The vendor gives no total, so size rests on reported reach.

Who is affected?

People and lawyers who use online case lookup, e-filing or reservation systems at courts that use the vendor's portal software. Courts say to file in person and to call clerk's offices meanwhile. Courts' own case-management systems are said to be separate from the portals.

Known so far

  • Journal Technologies says suspicious, malicious automated activity targeted its public portal software, first identified on Nov 13. evidence
  • The vendor advised customers to take their public portals offline, and courts did. evidence
  • Public portals or e-filing were offline at many courts, including Alameda, Kern, Marin, Ventura, Riverside, Los Angeles, Spokane, Lake County, Illinois, and King County, Washington. evidence
  • Kern's court says its portal is offline and gives no estimated restoration time. evidence
  • Riverside's court says its portal is restored and users must reset their passwords. evidence

Not known yet

  • Who was behind the activity.
  • Whether any data was exposed; the vendor says it has found no evidence so far for Criminal Justice Information and is still investigating.
  • When every affected portal will be restored, and how many courts are affected.
  • Whether this is connected to the other outages reported this week.
