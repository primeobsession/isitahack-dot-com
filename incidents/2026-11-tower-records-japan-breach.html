How big is it?

Large. About 11 million records of customer data, from a retailer's online shop, per the company. Counts overlap and the number of distinct people is not published.

Who is affected?

Tower Records members, including some who left within three years. Leaked: email, member ID, password hash, and for some, name, birth date, address or phone. Card data was not, per the company.

Known so far

  • An outside party got into Tower Records Online without authorisation on Nov 19. evidence
  • The company stopped the shop at about 5:10 PM JST and it is still offline for checks. evidence
  • About 11 million records leaked, including people who left within three years. evidence
  • 10,214,652 login records: email, member ID and password hash. evidence
  • No credit card information was included, per the company. evidence

Not known yet

  • How the attacker got in. open question
  • Who the attacker was. open question
  • Whether the data has been used against customers. open question
