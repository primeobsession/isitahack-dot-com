Was the Wellington IT credit union breach a hack?

Also reported as the Irish credit union data breach and the CU Online breach.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes: an outside party got unauthorised access to saved-payee details on Wellington IT's banking platform, per the department, Wellington IT and the credit union league. Money taken and the attacker are not established.

1
ConfirmedHackers got unauthorised access to saved-payee data on Wellington IT's platformWellington IT, its credit union customers and Ireland's Department of Finance describe an incident in which an outside party exploited a vulnerability in Wellington IT's banking platform and reached saved-payee details.

Why it supports A Tier 1 government statement, plus RTÉ, The Record and The Irish Times as independent outlets.

The Department of Finance statement describes an incident involving payee details, with the vulnerability identified and closed. RTÉ reports Wellington IT said hackers gained unauthorised access; The Record and The Irish Times report the same. The Irish Examiner, which saw Wellington's Nov 16 correspondence, gives about five million requests instead (c017); the two figures have not been reconciled.
