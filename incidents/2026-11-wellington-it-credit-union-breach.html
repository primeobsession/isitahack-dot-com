Was the Wellington IT credit union breach a hack?

Also reported as the Irish credit union data breach and the CU Online breach.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes: an outside party got unauthorised access to saved-payee details on Wellington IT's banking platform, per the department, Wellington IT and the credit union league. Money taken and the attacker are not established.

What happened?

ReportedWellington IT told credit unions about 1.4 million malicious requestsWellington IT told credit unions on Nov 16 that a 'bad actor' made some 1.4 million malicious requests and retrieved saved payee records (The Irish Times). The league's chief put the activity at Oct 2 to 4.

Why it supports Wellington's email and the league's account, relayed by three outlets, all from Wellington's information.

The Irish Times says the weakness was exploited on Oct 6, but Capital and Wexford credit unions' own notices, relaying Wellington, say it was exploited Oct 2 to 4 and closed Oct 6, matching the league and The Register. We use Oct 2 to 4 for the activity and Oct 6 for the fix. The Irish Examiner, which saw Wellington's Nov 16 correspondence, gives about five million requests instead (c017); the two figures have not been reconciled.

Wellington IT's disclosures list payee names, bank names, IBANs, BICs, account numbers, sort codes, addresses and payment references. The Register adds saved nicknames and internal reference numbers that do not identify customers.

Notice dated Nov 18.

Why it supports Nineteen credit unions' own notices (Tier 1), including North East, Capital, Wexford, Malahide, Phoenix, Member First and North Midlands, list the payee fields; RTÉ, The Register and The Irish Times report the same.

New Ross Credit Union lists payee name and saved nickname, IBAN, BIC, payee address and payment reference. Cara Credit Union lists the payee's account name and nickname, BIC, IBAN, payment reference and internal reference numbers, says payee addresses were affected only in a limited number of instances, and says email addresses and phone numbers were not in the data. Core, Gateway and Life credit unions list the same payee fields.
