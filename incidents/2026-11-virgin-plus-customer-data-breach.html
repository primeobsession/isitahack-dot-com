The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 9:40 PM ET

Virgin Plus says an unknown party accessed customer data on Nov 17. Confirmed by its notice; scale unknown.

How big is it?

Large. Virgin Plus has not said how many customers were affected. Large rests on reach, not a count: it is a national consumer brand of a major carrier. Revisit when a count is published.

Who is affected?

Virgin Plus customers whose records were in the internal system. The company says it is informing affected customers.

Known so far

  • Virgin Plus says an unknown party gained unauthorized access to an internal customer-data system on Nov 17. evidence
  • The data may include name, address, phone, email, account number, product information and last payment date and amount. evidence
  • Bell Canada reported its own separate breach of enterprise customer data on Nov 17. evidence

Not known yet

  • How many customers were affected. open question
  • Who was behind it. open question
  • Whether it is the same attack as Bell Canada's Nov 17 breach. open question
  • How the intruder got in.
