Was the Workday outage a hack?

Also reported as the Workday outage and the Workday payroll outage.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Not established

Not established. Workday says it was not a cyber incident and describes a fault in its own disaster-recovery setup. No independent source confirms that, and Workday has published no root-cause report.

Rests on Workday said the outage was not related to a cybersecurity incident · Workday's customer notice: its disaster-recovery site unexpectedly connected to production databases · Disaster Recovery Journal: none of five outages in four days was a cyberattack, and Workday's DR environment triggered its outage

This is the current answer, not a final verdict. A final verdict is published only after the site's owner has reviewed the evidence.
