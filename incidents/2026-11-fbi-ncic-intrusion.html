The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 8:19 AM ET

The Director of National Intelligence says the FBI 'has an incident' it is handling, but gives no details; the FBI has not commented.

How big is it?

Nation-level potential. NCIC is used by more than 100,000 criminal justice partners nationwide. No source says what was reached, so this is the worst case, not a known impact.

Who is affected?

Potentially police and courts that rely on NCIC, and people with records in it. No source says whose records, if any, were reached, and there is no notice for the public to act on.

Known so far

  • CNN, NBC News, AP, ABC News, MS NOW and Fox News, each citing their own unnamed sources, report the FBI is investigating an apparent breach of NCIC. evidence
  • NCIC is the FBI-run database police nationwide use for warrants, stolen property and other records. evidence
  • The FBI has not commented on the reports. evidence
  • the President called the reported hack 'not good' on Nov 21 but gave no details. evidence
  • The Director of National Intelligence said on Nov 22 the FBI 'has an incident' it is handling, without details. evidence
  • A separate breach hit the FBI jobs site in September; the FBI blamed a contractor's missed patch. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether the FBI confirms the breach. The reports rest on unnamed sources, and the President's and the DNI's remarks gave no details.
  • What part of NCIC was reached, how the intruders got in, and who they are.
  • Whether any records were copied or changed.
  • Whether it is linked to this week's outages or the September jobs-site breach. No source says so.
  • Whether NCIC service for police was interrupted.
