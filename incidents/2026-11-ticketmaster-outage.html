  • Ticketmaster said at 12:34 AM ET on Saturday, Nov 21, that tickets were displaying properly again. It did not say what went wrong. evidence

  • Ticketmaster failures also delayed entry at a concert and a music festival in Mexico City that night, according to the venue and the promoter. evidence
  • On Thursday, Nov 19, a Ticketmaster outage held up entry at the South Dakota state volleyball tournament, according to the tournament's organizer. evidence

Not known yet

  • What caused the outage. Ticketmaster has not said.
  • Whether it was a cyberattack. No one has claimed one, reported one, or ruled one out.
  • Whether it is linked to the Nov 19 disruption or to other outages this month.
  • How many people were affected.
  • Whether fans who missed the Nov 20 show will get refunds. WWL asked Ticketmaster and has not reported an answer.
