What happened?

7
ConfirmedA Ticketmaster outage stopped fans at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans from loading mobile tickets on Friday night, Nov 20Fans at the first night of the Usher and Chris Brown show could not load Ticketmaster tickets. The organizer, Live Nation New Orleans, and the venue manager, ASM Global, both said Ticketmaster was having an outage.

Why it supports Four outlets with different owners report the organizer's and the venue manager's own statements, and WDSU had crews on site.

Live Nation New Orleans posted that Ticketmaster was 'currently experiencing a service outage' and told fans to go to the box office. Fans who had saved tickets to their phone wallets got in without trouble. Doors were set for 6 PM Central (7 PM ET).
