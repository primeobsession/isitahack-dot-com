Incidents / Rackspace Spot breach, Nov 2026 · Incident date Nov 17, 2026 · First published Nov 21, 2026

Was Rackspace Spot hacked?

Also reported as the Rackspace Spot outage and the Rackspace Spot Kubernetes breach.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Reported

Reportedly yes: Rackspace's customer notice, quoted by BleepingComputer, says leaked credentials let someone into Spot on Nov 17, and a Spot team member said the same on Spot's GitHub forum. No Rackspace-hosted statement yet.

Rests on Rackspace told customers an unauthorized party used leaked credentials to access parts of Rackspace Spot on Nov 17 · Rackspace says the unauthorized party could act as legitimate users in some customer organizations: create pods in Kubernetes clusters, read Kubernetes Secrets, and list nodes and volumes · Customer data was stolen or misused · A Spot team member said on Spot's official GitHub Discussions forum that an observability system was breached using a compromised credential

This is the current answer, not a final verdict. A final verdict is published only after the site's owner has reviewed the evidence.

04

What happened?

4
ReportedRackspace says the unauthorized party could act as legitimate users in some customer organizations: create pods in Kubernetes clusters, read Kubernetes Secrets, and list nodes and volumesPer the notice as BleepingComputer reports it, attackers could read any Secret in an affected user's clusters, plus any Spot API or refresh tokens that user held. Rackspace learned of it Nov 18 after a customer report.

Why it supports Only BleepingComputer's account of the private notice supports it.

'Some customer organizations' is Rackspace's wording; it did not say how many. Rackspace says 'could have read', which is not a finding that any Secret was read.

  • BleepingComputer: Rackspace Spot breach let hackers access customer Kubernetes secrets (quotes customer notice) News outlet · Nov 21, 2026

Last checked Nov 21, 2026, 6:34 PM ET

ReportedThe Spot admin console was down for several days while Rackspace locked the platform downBleepingComputer says the breach caused an extended Spot admin console outage that stopped customers rotating credentials. It quotes a Spot co-developer on Reddit saying access was shut down deliberately to sanitize the environment.

Why it supports Outlet report, plus a Spot team member's forum post (Tier 4) describing the deliberate shutdown; neither is a Rackspace-hosted statement.

The Reddit post is quoted second-hand and we did not read it. On Nov 20 a Spot team member wrote on Spot's official GitHub Discussions forum that, because of the nature of the incident, the team was making several intrusive changes to the underlying infrastructure clusters, causing the extended outage. That is a forum post, not a Rackspace statement. Rackspace's own status page and release notes do not describe the outage.
