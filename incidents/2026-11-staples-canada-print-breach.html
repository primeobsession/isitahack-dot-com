Was the Staples Canada print website breach a hack?

Also reported as the staplesprint.ca breach and the Staples Print hack.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, by Staples' own account: an unknown party got into staplesprint.ca without permission between Nov 1 and Nov 5 and had access to customer information. Who did it is not known.
