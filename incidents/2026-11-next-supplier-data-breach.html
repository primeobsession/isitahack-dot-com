How big is it?

Large. No count of affected people applies. Large rests on potential reach, not a measured impact: Next has 800+ stores in the UK and Ireland and sells online, and the supplier is unnamed. Next says no customer personal data is known to be affected. Revisit if more is disclosed.

Who is affected?

Next, whose data a supplier held. Next says no customer personal data is known to be affected; the supplier is unnamed and its other customers are unknown. Be wary of unexpected emails, texts or calls claiming to be from Next, as an outside security firm advised.
