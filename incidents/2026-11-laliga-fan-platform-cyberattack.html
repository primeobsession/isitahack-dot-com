The latest · Nov 23, 2026, 6:12 AM ET

LALIGA says an attacker extracted fan data from a platform on Nov 14 to 16. Confirmed by its statement; scale and cause unknown.

How big is it?

Large. LALIGA has not said how many people were affected. Large rests on potential reach, not a count: a national league whose fan platform also serves some clubs. Revisit when a count is published.

Who is affected?

Users registered in digital services of LALIGA and of some clubs, per LALIGA. How many is not stated.

Known so far

  • LALIGA says it suffered a cyberattack on one of its fan-services platforms. evidence
  • LALIGA says personal data of registered users was extracted. evidence
