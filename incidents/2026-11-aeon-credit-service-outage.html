Was AEON Credit Service hacked?

Also reported as the AEON credit card outage and the AEON Credit Service hack.

Is it a hack? The short answer

Confirmed

Yes, by the company's own notice: AEON says it detected unauthorized access to its system. It has not said that this caused the outage, how the access happened, or who did it.

Rests on AEON says its system was accessed without authorization · AEON acted to contain the incident and hired outside forensics experts

This is the current answer, not a final verdict. A final verdict is published only after the site's owner has reviewed the evidence.

What happened?

4
ConfirmedThe card portal and app were offline for more than 40 hours from Nov 18AEON's online services and mobile app showed 'system maintenance' from the evening of Wed Nov 18; Dimsum Daily reported them still down, more than 40 hours in, at 2 PM on Fri Nov 20 (HK time).

Why it supports Several outlets with different owners report the dates; Dimsum Daily's Nov 20 article gives the 40-hour count as of 2 PM that day, and The Standard, Metro Radio and the Hong Kong Economic Journal give Nov 21 as the day services came back.

The Standard, Metro Radio, the Hong Kong Economic Journal and Sing Tao Headline say services resumed on Saturday, Nov 21. Cardholders could not see statements or repay online, and some could not finish app-based checks for online shopping. Corrected Nov 22: the 40-hour count was as of Nov 20, not Nov 21.
