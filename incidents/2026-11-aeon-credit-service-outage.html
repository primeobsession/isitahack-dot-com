How much do you want to read?

The latest · Nov 22, 2026, 4:34 PM ET

Hong Kong's privacy office reportedly says AEON had not notified it of a data breach as of Nov 22.

How big is it?

Local. One Hong Kong credit card lender. AEON has not said how many customers were affected; its portal and app were down for more than 40 hours.

Who is affected?

AEON credit card customers in Hong Kong, who could not use the online portal and app from Nov 18. AEON says affected customers will be told individually.

Known so far

  • AEON says its system was accessed without authorization. evidence
  • The portal and app were offline for more than 40 hours from the evening of Nov 18. evidence
  • AEON hired outside cybersecurity and forensics experts. evidence
  • AEON says it has found no evidence any data was misused so far. evidence

Not known yet

  • Whether the intrusion caused the outage.
  • What data was reached, and how many customers.
  • How the intruder got in, or who it was.
  • Whether any regulator is looking into it.
