The latest · Nov 24, 2026, 7:30 AM ET

Spotify's Nov 24 login outage lasted about an hour. Spotify has not given a cause, and nobody has reported a hack.

How big is it?

Large. Spotify users in at least a dozen countries reported login problems, with more than 16,500 Downdetector reports in the US alone. Spotify has not said how many people were affected.

Who is affected?

Spotify users on the morning of Nov 24 (US time) who were logged out and could not sign back in. Sign-ins were working again for many by about 7:15 AM ET. Users still having trouble can contact Spotify support.

Known so far

  • On the morning of Nov 24, many Spotify users were logged out and could not sign back in. evidence
  • Spotify's status account said it was aware of issues and checking them out. It gave no cause. evidence

Not known yet

  • What caused the outage. Spotify has not said. open question
  • Whether it was a cyberattack. No one has claimed one, reported one, or ruled one out.
  • How many people were affected. open question
  • When the problem was fully fixed. Spotify has not given an end time.

Not verified: initial investigation only. Nothing on this page is absolutely confirmed. It is an early look based only on publicly available sources, and it should not be treated as verified fact. Even items labeled "Confirmed" only mean a public source reports them.
